## Critical Thinking (Q431–Q440) — 10 questions

**Q431.** A study shows that students who eat breakfast score higher on tests. Which conclusion is most valid?

   A. Eating breakfast causes better test scores
   B. There may be a correlation between breakfast and test scores
   C. Students who don't eat breakfast are less intelligent
   D. All students should be required to eat breakfast
Correct Answer: **B** — There may be a correlation between breakfast and test scores

**Q432.** "All birds can fly. Penguins are birds. Therefore, penguins can fly." What's wrong with this reasoning?

   A. The conclusion doesn't follow from the premises
   B. The first statement is false
   C. The second statement is false
   D. Nothing is wrong with the reasoning
Correct Answer: **B** — The first statement is false

**Q433.** If Store A has lower prices than Store B, and Store B has lower prices than Store C, then:

   A. Store A definitely has the lowest prices overall
   B. Store C has the highest prices of the three
   C. Store B has average prices
   D. We need more information to compare all stores
Correct Answer: **B** — Store C has the highest prices of the three

**Q434.** Which statement best evaluates this argument: "Most successful people wake up early. Therefore, if you want to be successful, you should wake up early."

   A. The argument is logically sound
   B. The argument assumes correlation implies causation
   C. The argument is based on false premises
   D. The argument is supported by sufficient evidence
Correct Answer: **B** — The argument assumes correlation implies causation

**Q435.** A survey found that 80% of people prefer chocolate ice cream. Which factor would most likely affect the validity of this conclusion?

   A. The survey method used
   B. The sample size and selection
   C. The time of year the survey was conducted
   D. All of the above
Correct Answer: **D** — All of the above

**Q436.** "If it rains, the picnic will be cancelled. The picnic was cancelled. Therefore, it rained." This reasoning is:

   A. Valid and sound
   B. Valid but not sound
   C. Invalid
   D. Cannot be determined
Correct Answer: **C** — Invalid

**Q437.** Which source would be most reliable for information about climate change?

   A. A blog post by someone who experienced unusual weather
   B. A peer-reviewed scientific journal article
   C. A social media post with lots of shares
   D. A news article quoting various opinions
Correct Answer: **B** — A peer-reviewed scientific journal article

**Q438.** Examine this claim: "Video games cause violence because many violent criminals play video games." What's the main flaw in this reasoning?

   A. It's based on insufficient data
   B. It confuses correlation with causation
   C. It uses emotional rather than logical appeals
   D. It makes an unfair generalization
Correct Answer: **B** — It confuses correlation with causation

**Q439.** If a medication is effective for 85% of patients, and a patient doesn't improve after taking it, what can we conclude?

   A. The medication doesn't work
   B. The patient is in the 15% for whom it's not effective
   C. The patient didn't take it correctly
   D. More information is needed to draw any conclusion
Correct Answer: **D** — More information is needed to draw any conclusion

**Q440.** Which question would best help evaluate the credibility of a news article?

   A. Is the headline attention-grabbing?
   B. Are multiple credible sources cited?
   C. Does it confirm what I already believe?
   D. Is it shared by many people on social media?
